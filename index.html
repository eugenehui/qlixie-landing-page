Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a one-time purchase or a subscription?

One unlock gives you full access to all colors, shapes, sounds, haptics, and features listed above. No subscriptions, no surprise paywalls.

Does Qlixie require an internet connection?

Not at all. Qlixie works 100% offline, making it perfect for airplane mode, subways, or anywhere you need a moment of focus without signal.

Does using haptics and sound drain the battery?

Qlixie is built natively with Swift to be ultra-lightweight and battery-efficient. It uses standard iOS Taptic Engine impulses, which use minimal power—comparable to standard typing on your phone.

Is Qlixie a system keyboard replacement?

No, Qlixie is a standalone interactive key and sound environment app designed for fidgeting, ASMR, and tactile satisfaction. It does not replace your system keyboard for typing messages in other apps.

Do you track my taps or personal data?

Never. Qlixie does not collect, store, or transmit any data. Everything happens entirely on your device.
